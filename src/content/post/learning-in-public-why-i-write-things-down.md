I learn best by doing — taking something apart, breaking it, and figuring out why. But doing isn't enough on its own. The lesson fades fast unless I write it down.

## Why bother writing it down

- **It forces clarity.** If I can't explain it simply, I don't really understand it yet.
- **It compounds.** Notes from six months ago save me hours today. Past me is a surprisingly helpful colleague.
- **It's how I share.** Half the things I know, I learned from someone else's write-up. This blog is me paying that back.

## What you'll find here

This is where I'll post what I'm learning — AI and IT in semiconductor manufacturing, smart-home projects, DIY experiments, and the occasional thing that simply caught my interest. Some posts will be polished; some will just be notes. Both are useful.

Thanks for reading. If something here is helpful — or wrong — I'd genuinely like to hear about it.
